# Digital marketing hero animation

The standard-page hero star is replaced with an original vector megaphone, floating reaction/chat bubbles, analytics card, orbit and pulsing broadcast marks. Gradient shading, perspective rotation, shadows and independent floating motion create a dimensional scene. No new dependencies, external assets or WebGL contexts are needed.

Desktop uses a two-column composition. Mobile places the artwork below the headline and actions. Reduced motion retains the static illustration. The artwork is decorative, hidden from screen readers, pointer-transparent and prerendered with the page.
